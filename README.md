# MaxIPTV-Releases
Lightweight IPTV Player (In French for the moment, sorry international language maybe later)
Simply put your Xtream codes in the "connexion" bouton, choose your profile (customizable), and roam freely in your provider Live Tv, film and series catalog.
Favorites and resume film or series, by profile.
Have fun and tell me if you found some issues or way to enhance it.
